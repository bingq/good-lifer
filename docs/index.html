Good Lifer

A calm, private diary for iPhone, iPad and Mac. One page for each day. Your pages stay on your devices.

What it does

  • One page per dayOpen the app and today's page is ready. Add a mood, a place and tags, then just write.
  • CalendarSee your month at a glance, with each day's mood and first line.
  • iPhone, iPad and MacA native app on each device. Your diary syncs through your own iCloud Drive.
  • Plain files you ownEvery day is a normal Markdown file in a "Good Lifer" folder in iCloud Drive. Open it in Files or Finder, back it up, or take it anywhere.
  • Private by designNo account, no ads, no tracking. We never see your diary.

Where are my entries stored?

When iCloud Drive is on, in a folder called Good Lifer in your iCloud Drive. It's private to your Apple account. When iCloud Drive is off, the entries stay on that device only.

How do I sync between iPhone and Mac?

Sign in to the same Apple account on each device and turn on iCloud Drive. Entries written on one device appear on the others a few moments later.

What happens if I edit the same day on two devices?

Nothing is lost. If both versions changed while offline, Good Lifer keeps both, separated by a line, so you can tidy them up.

How do I back up my diary?

Your entries are ordinary files. Copy the Good Lifer folder from iCloud Drive (or the Files app) anywhere you like.

I used the earlier version of Good Lifer. What changed?

Version 1.0 is a complete redesign: Good Lifer is now a diary for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Data from the earlier achievement-tracking version isn't carried over.

If you have entries you want to keep, open the earlier version before updating and use Settings → Data Management → Export Data.
