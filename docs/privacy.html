Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Good Lifer is a private diary. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. Good Lifer has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server that receives your data and cannot see your diary.

Where your diary is stored

  • On your devices. Each diary page is saved as a file on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  • In your own iCloud Drive, if you turn it on. To sync between devices, Good Lifer saves your pages in a "Good Lifer" folder in your iCloud Drive. That storage belongs to your Apple account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Sharing

Good Lifer does not share, sell or send your data to anyone. If you copy or share a diary file yourself, that's up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete an entry by clearing its page, or delete the Good Lifer folder in iCloud Drive and the Files app. Deleting the app removes the copies stored on that device.

Children

Good Lifer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
